Quick thought on grain

October 22, 2025

Texture changes everything.

Perfectly smooth design feels like a product. Sterile. Factory-optimized. Inoffensive. Works. Nobody hates.

Nobody loves either.

Grain says human. Hands. Care. Imperfection. Pixels that don't line up perfectly. Colors slightly off.

Not broken. Just alive.

I tried grain everywhere. Backgrounds. Type. Edges. Work felt different suddenly. Not something made. Something made by someone.

Grain is the fingerprint.

Sounds small. Difference between design and craft. Brand decision vs person. Cold vs warm.

Proud work always has grain. Not slick. Texture. I was here. I made this. My imperfections baked in.

That's the difference between designed and made.
